IT Governance: A CEO's Quick Guide

Lumaveris insight · 4 min read

← All insights

Good governance does not slow an organisation down. It makes better decisions easier to repeat.

Technology leadership is about creating enough clarity that people can act with confidence, risks are visible early, and change remains connected to organisational goals.

Set clear decision rights

Everyone involved in technology should understand who decides, who advises, and who is accountable.

Measure the outcomes

Governance is strongest when it focuses on value, resilience, and delivery outcomes.

Keep it proportionate

Simple, repeatable guardrails let teams move faster while escalating the choices that deserve greater scrutiny.
